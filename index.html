Payment

Pay to secure your slot

  1. Book your day and time in the calendar above.
  2. Work out your total: the hourly rate times the number of hours. Example: 2 hours on a weekday evening is ₱1,000.
  3. Open GCash, tap Pay QR and scan the code. Check that the name shown matches the name on the code. Booking on the same phone? Save or screenshot the code and choose Upload QR in GCash.
  4. Enter the amount and put your name and your booking date and time in the message.
  5. Send the receipt screenshot to pcklblock@gmail.com.

Your slot is secured once we receive your payment. Transfer fees may apply.

When are you open?

Every hour of every day. Pickleball anytime you want, how you want it.

How do I book a court?

Pick a day and start time in the booking calendar and enter your name and email. You get a confirmation and a calendar invite right away. Weekday and weekend rates apply.

How do I pay for a booking?

After you book, pay by GCash. Scan the QR code in the booking section, send the amount for your hours, and email the receipt screenshot to pcklblock@gmail.com. Your slot is secured once we receive payment.

Do I need my own paddle?

No. Paddles can be rented at the venue for ₱100 each. Tell us in the note on your request and we will have them ready. Nelson CZ-40 balls are also for sale at ₱100 each.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. You can go to your email confirmation to cancel or change your appointment. If you need assistance, please email us at pcklblock@gmail.com.
